How do I play?

Swipe left or right to change lanes, swipe up or tap to jump, and swipe down to drop quickly. Your fuse burns from 30 seconds. At each gate, take the green box (+ time) or jump for the gold box (more time). Hit a hurdle or run out of fuse and you explode.

There's no sound.

Make sure Silent Mode is off and the volume is up. Then check that Sound and Music are ticked on the Boom Lane start screen.

How do I get rid of the ads?

Buy Remove Ads in the game, or survive 5:00 on Pro without reviving. Ads you choose to watch, to revive or double your coins, stay available.

I lost my coins or scores.

Progress is saved on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes it, and it doesn't move to a new phone. A "Remove Ads" purchase is tied to your Apple account and can be restored. If you need help, email us.

I was charged but the ads are still showing.

Email us with the date of the purchase. Refunds for App Store purchases are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I found a bug or have an idea.

We'd love to hear it. Email us with a screenshot or a short description of what happened.
