BOOM LANE · IPHONE GAME

Privacy Policy

Effective 2 November 2026 · Published by SHEIKH RAHEEL MAJEED

In short: Boom Lane has no accounts and we don't collect your name, email or contacts. Your progress stays on your phone. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google may collect some device data to show them, as described below.

WHO WE ARE

Boom Lane is published by SHEIKH RAHEEL MAJEED. If you have a question about this policy or your data, email contact@holglobal.ca

WHAT STAYS ON YOUR DEVICE

The game saves your progress on your iPhone so you can pick up where you left off:

  • best scores and best Pro-challenge time for each level
  • coins, the ball skins you own and the one you use
  • daily mission progress
  • settings such as sound, music and your chosen level
  • whether you have unlocked the ad-free version

This information is not sent to us. Deleting the app deletes it.

ADS

The free version of Boom Lane shows ads from Google AdMob: full-screen ads between runs, and optional video ads you choose to watch to revive or double your coins. To show and measure these ads, Google may collect:

  • device identifiers, including Apple's advertising identifier, but only if you allow tracking when iOS asks
  • your IP address, which can indicate your approximate location
  • information about the ads you see and tap
  • diagnostic data such as crashes and performance

If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", ads are not personalised using your advertising identifier. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Players in the European Economic Area and the UK are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown.

Google's use of this data is covered by How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

PURCHASES

The optional "Remove Ads" purchase is handled by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. Apple tells the app only whether the purchase was made, so the ads can be switched off. Apple's handling is covered by the Apple Privacy Policy.

LAUNCH UPDATES

If you sign up on our website to hear when Boom Lane launches, we keep the email address you give us in a Google Form and use it only to tell you about Boom Lane. We don't sell it or share it. Email us to have it removed at any time.

CHILDREN

Boom Lane is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, email us and we will delete it.

YOUR CHOICES

  • Turn off ad tracking in your iPhone's Tracking settings.
  • Remove ads with the "Remove Ads" purchase, or by beating the 5:00 challenge on Pro.
  • Delete the app to delete all game data stored on your phone.
  • Email us to ask about or delete any information we hold about you.

CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

If we change how Boom Lane handles data, we will update this page and the date at the top before the change takes effect.
